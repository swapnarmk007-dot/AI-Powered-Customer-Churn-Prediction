CUSTOMER SUPPORT STRATEGIES

1. Technical Support
Provide quick assistance for internet, network, and service-related problems.

2. Customer Complaints
Listen to customer complaints and resolve issues as quickly as possible.

3. 24/7 Support
Provide support channels to help customers when they experience problems.

4. Customer Communication
Keep customers informed about service updates, maintenance, and changes.

5. Problem Resolution
Identify recurring customer problems and develop permanent solutions.

6. Customer Experience
Provide friendly, professional, and helpful customer service.

7. Follow-Up
Contact customers after resolving complaints to check their satisfaction.
