CUSTOMER PRICING AND DISCOUNT STRATEGIES

1. Competitive Pricing
Maintain competitive pricing compared with similar service providers.

2. Discount Offers
Provide suitable discounts to eligible customers.

3. Contract Discounts
Offer attractive pricing for customers choosing long-term contracts.

4. Personalized Pricing
Understand customer usage patterns before recommending suitable service plans.

5. Loyalty Discounts
Reward long-term customers with eligible loyalty benefits.

6. Transparent Billing
Ensure customers understand monthly charges, additional fees, and billing policies.

7. Flexible Plans
Provide different service plans according to customer requirements and budgets.
