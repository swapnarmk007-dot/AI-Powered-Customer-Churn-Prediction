CUSTOMER RETENTION STRATEGIES

1. Customer Loyalty Programs
Offer loyalty rewards to customers who continue using company services.

2. Long-Term Contracts
Provide suitable discounts for customers who choose one-year or two-year contracts.

3. Personalized Offers
Analyze customer usage and provide relevant offers based on customer needs.

4. Customer Engagement
Regularly communicate with customers through email, messages, and support calls.

5. Customer Feedback
Collect customer feedback to understand dissatisfaction and improve services.

6. Churn Prevention
Identify customers with a high likelihood of leaving and contact them proactively.

7. Customer Satisfaction
Improve service quality, reliability, and customer experience.
